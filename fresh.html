Force an update

For when the app keeps opening an old build no matter how many times you close and reopen it.

Your data is not touched Shifts, settings and your signature live in this browser's local storage. This page does not read or write any of it, and does not affect any other app published on this domain. It removes only the cached copy of the app itself.
What this does Unregisters the service worker holding the old page, empties its caches, then reopens the app so it loads fresh from the server.
